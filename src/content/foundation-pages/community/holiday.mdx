## **Celebrate the Holiday Season with Interledger! Participate in Our Holiday Card and Sticker Contest.**

<br />
We are calling on all artists, builders, and community members to design our
official 2026 end-of-year Holiday card and sticker. Winning designs will be
printed and mailed directly to Interledger members across the globe in December!
Submissions are open from October 22 - November 13.
<br />

</Paragraph>

### How This Works

Entries are accepted across two categories: **Holiday Sticker** and **Holiday Card**. To ensure inclusive holiday messaging across global cultures, all submissions must align with an inclusive "end of year" theme (e.g. no Christmas-specific imagery). 

Templates and layout guides can be downloaded directly from the Design Kit repository; see below.

</Paragraph>

## **Official Competition Rules & Policies**

### **Theme & Cultural Inclusivity**

- **Campaign:** "End-of-Year Celebration"
- **Theme:** Connectedness, open technology, new beginnings, global nodes
- Designs must focus on global reflection, gratitude, community, and looking forward to the new year.
- **Exclusions:** Avoid specific religious-only imagery to ensure the designs are inclusive across cultures, holidays, and secular celebrations worldwide.<br /> 

Please read our [AI Usage Policy, Intellectual Property and Printing Rights](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1INWzKmaY5Pv_YmFa4z5Jz9fNh8xMqiJHZUY7LeoXcIM/edit?disco=doco.-HnBUlWSNwrJPrm_WkxeiQ&tab=t.0).

</Paragraph>

## **Judging Criteria & Selection Process**

Submissions will be evaluated through a two-stage selection process.

### **Stage 1: Internal Screening**

We will review all eligible entries and select 5-10 finalists per category based on the following rubric:

| **Criterion**                | **Weight** | **Evaluation Focus**                                                                    |
| ---------------------------- | ---------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Theme Alignment**          | 30%        | Captures end-of-year spirit, inclusivity, and global community connection without bias. |
| **Creativity & Originality** | 30%        | Unique visual interpretation, compelling composition, and personal artistic expression. |
| **Print Feasibility**        | 20%        | Meets technical specifications (300 DPI, dimensions, clarity).                          |
| **Versatility & Appeal**     | 20%        | Translates effectively across physical print formats and digital showcase spaces.       |

### **Stage 2: Community Voting**

- The top 5-10 finalist designs in each category will be published for public community voting.
- Voting will open on November 17 and close on November 23.
- Community members will vote via email form.

</Paragraph>
